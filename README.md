# GenMusic (formerly Piano Practice)

A browser app for practising piano on a digital piano connected over USB (Web MIDI).

- Shows the sheet music and lights up the keys to play next. It waits until every note of a chord is played.
- You can practise the right hand, the left hand or both, choose a range of bars, and loop it.
- Falling-notes game mode; the other hand plays along.
- Streaks with freeze days, stickers, and tricky-spot loops after each run.
- 🥁 Metronome: follows each piece's time signature and tempo (6/8 counts 2 beats per bar). Adjust it with −/+, a slider, 50%/75%, or tap tempo, and turn the beat-1 accent on or off.
- 🎙 Record yourself (chords included): play it back on the piano or download it as a MIDI file.
- Parent screen (hold 👪 for 3 seconds + 4-digit code): minutes per day, most-missed notes, progress per piece, settings, and export/import of progress between devices.
- Comes with a library of public-domain pieces at roughly Grade 1–4 level. You can add your own MusicXML (`.musicxml`, `.xml`, `.mxl`) or MIDI (`.mid`) files.
- Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer, and Chrome on Android. Safari and iPhone/iPad have no Web MIDI.

Progress is stored in the browser on each device. See CREDITS.md for score sources and licences.
